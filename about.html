About Sunrise Community Hospital

Our Story

Sunrise Community Hospital was founded with one simple goal: to make good healthcare accessible to everyone in our community. What began as a small clinic has grown into a full-service hospital offering outpatient, inpatient, and emergency care.

Our Mission

To provide safe, compassionate, and affordable healthcare to every patient who walks through our doors, while embracing technology to make our services easier to access - such as this website and our online appointment booking system.

Our Values

Compassion

We treat every patient with dignity, kindness, and respect.

Excellence

We hold ourselves to high standards of medical care.

Integrity

We are honest and transparent with our patients at all times.
